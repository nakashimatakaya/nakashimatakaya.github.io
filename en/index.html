02RESEARCH

Research & publications

My research examines treatment timing and patient trajectories, with a focus on sepsis and perioperative care.

01

Clinical epidemiology and causal inference

Observational studies using multicenter cohorts and clinical databases. I study treatment choices and outcomes using target trial emulation and marginal structural models.

02

Machine learning and prediction models

I develop prediction models using clinical data, with a focus on eGFR slope in chronic kidney disease.

03

Longitudinal data analysis

I use nonlinear mixed-effects models and functional data analysis to study how measurements such as lactate levels change over time.
