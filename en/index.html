About me

Takaya NakashimaNagasaki University Hospital

Physician, Department of Anesthesiology and Intensive Care Medicine; Research Associate, Clinical Research Center, Nagasaki University Hospital

I work on study design and statistical analysis at the Clinical Research Center. I am enrolled in the doctoral program at Nagasaki University Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences and collaborate with TXP Medical.

Selected research

Selected studies cover treatment timing, ICU registry research, and longitudinal methods. Each entry links to the original paper.

Intensive Care Medicine

2026 · First and corresponding author

Vasopressin initiation in septic shock

We compared ultra-early and early adjunctive vasopressin initiation after norepinephrine escalation using MIMIC-IV and eICU-CRD. We used target trial emulation with the clone-censor-weight method to compare the treatment strategies.

Target trial emulation · Clone-censor-weight · ICU databases

Journal of Anesthesia, Analgesia and Critical Care

2026 · First and corresponding author

Anaphylactic shock in Japanese ICUs

Using JIPAD, we described the clinical characteristics, management, and outcomes of 643 adult ICU admissions with registry-identified anaphylactic shock. The study covers fiscal years 2015–2023 at participating Japanese ICUs.

Multicenter registry · Descriptive epidemiology · JIPAD

Read the paper↗

International Heart Journal

2024 · First and corresponding author

Lactate trajectories in acute cardiovascular disease

We used functional data analysis based on nonlinear mixed-effects models to estimate the timeframe of lactate reduction in adults with acute cardiovascular disease.

Functional data analysis · Nonlinear mixed-effects models

Read the paper↗

Research focus & methods

01

Clinical epidemiology and causal inference

I use target trial emulation, the clone-censor-weight method, and marginal structural models to study treatment strategies using observational data.

02

Machine learning and prediction models

I develop prediction models using clinical data, with a focus on eGFR slope in chronic kidney disease.

03

Longitudinal data analysis

I use nonlinear mixed-effects models and functional data analysis to study how measurements such as lactate levels change over time.
